Companion Cultivation UI audio
================================

Source
------

Their use in Companion Cultivation and the edits below were explicitly approved
in the project conversation on 2026-11-17. No separate licence file was supplied.

Processing
----------
All game copies are 48 kHz Ogg Vorbis (quality 5). Short fades were added at
clip boundaries to prevent clicks. Original recordings remain untouched.

- buying.mp3: retained the first event only (0.00-1.35 s).
- page-turning.wav: split into ten independent page turns at
  0.000-0.400, 1.390-2.020, 2.520-3.200, 3.820-4.450,
  5.140-5.880, 7.230-7.840, 8.410-8.990, 9.820-10.370,
  11.220-11.780, and 12.240-12.736 s.
- pencil writing.wav: retained seconds 2, 3, and 4 (2.00-5.00 s).
- shop open and close.wav: opening is 0.52-3.18 s; closing is 5.50-8.20 s.
- remove item from hex.flac: resampled from 11.025 kHz and given a 0.04 s
  silent fade tail so its unusually short stream closes cleanly in browsers.
- taskboard.mp3: split into all 21 events in source order. Odd events are the
  eleven opening variants; even events are the ten closing variants.

Taskboard event ranges in source order:
0.08-0.70, 1.38-2.08, 2.80-3.55, 4.23-4.55, 5.20-5.87,
6.80-7.60, 8.64-8.95, 9.45-10.14, 11.01-11.66, 12.88-13.55,
14.51-15.30, 17.18-17.87, 19.25-19.88, 21.77-22.45,
24.04-24.69, 27.03-27.80, 29.88-30.58, 32.94-33.68,
34.83-36.10, 37.35-38.10, and 39.99-40.85 s.
