The running game is **Prototype 12 — player/developer UI separation**. **The full approved first-pass organism and route rosters have been implemented**, and Prototype 11's distinct Leaf inventory is retained. This remains a mechanics-testing garden, **not** a claim of settled balance, exhaustive layout feasibility, art, UI or full release completeness.

**Next agreed design, not implemented:** [200-slot spatial inventory, immediately repeatable staging, Apply, delayed concealed germination and drag-to-harvest](design/systems/spatial-inventory-and-staging.md). The user has explicitly required that **simulation time and visible existing-organism Growth/Transformations continue while any staging phase is open**. The current interface still displays inventory as a list, spends Seeds/Spores and reveals results on immediate sowing, harvests by an inspector button, and advances time only with developer controls. Do not confuse approved upcoming behavior with existing runnable coverage.

## What is playable now

- **Hex environment:** Moisture, Temperature and Illumination baselines; configurable ordinary/anchored Sources; additive and routed Spread Budget emissions; blocker-aware finite Airborne and connected-Flooded Waterborne reach; environmental adjacency and inspection. These remain the established [environment rules](design/systems/environment.md).
- **Organisms:** Sow owned Seeds or Spores in the ordinary workbench. Turn on **Display developer tools** beside the logo to place any of the 60 registered Plants or 31 registered Fungi for debugging, bypassing sowing. Living Plants advance through Growth (0–100%), Ripening (100–200%) and Mature; Fungi use Growth, Sporulation and Mature. The developer-only `+1 hour` and `+1 day` buttons advance the deterministic simulation. Fungi provide separate environmental emissions and cultivation Effects.
- **Farming economy:** **Each Plant produces a separate named Leaf item** (`inventory.leaves[plantSpecies]`); there is no pooled Leaf currency. Seed/Spore inventories, sowing attempts that spend stock, condition- and Effect-sensitive Growth/outputs, time-based output accrual, recipient compatibility, Seed identities, 3:1 net Allocation, one destructive harvest and Spores remain functional. A Plant's Leaves keep their source identity even if it produces another species' Seeds; Allocation changes quantity, never Leaf type. Fresh gardens start with 16 each of Potato, Daisydaze, Aquatic Base, Woodlands Base, Jadeheart and **Thymely** Seeds; these are **prototype starting quantities**. The interface also offers free placement for test setups without spending stock. Mirage Rose can deliver two Seed species in the same harvest; Spices' different-species Seed identities are also active. A species with no approved plantable Seeds cannot be sown, even if an imported inventory incorrectly contains stock for it.
- **Mycelial ground:** Timed Mycelord expansion; impassable Flooded support connections; selective blockers, overlapping contributors, unsupported retreat; dependent-Fungus sowing requirements and habitat grace; Mycelord Flooded death. This overlay is playable, though its **durations are still provisional**.
- **Transformations:** Real, blocker-aware Fungal contact and resolved Condition checks; per-Plant uninterrupted clocks, fresh destination Plants, transformation/death exact-time tie handling and inspected recipe progress. All six families are registered, including 69 approved routes and event-sensitive transitions. See the tests listed below.
- **Persistence and UI:** Inspect Plant/Fungus stats and recipe eligibility; **see separate Leaf stacks for each producing Plant**; use a top-bar toggle to reveal/hide developer-only placements and mutations, while keeping Inspect, Sow Seeds, Sow Spores and Harvest visible. Inspector baseline editors, Source and Blocker configuration, free deletion, time skipping, demo/blank layout, grid resizing and JSON import/export are test-only. Hiding the tools resets an active debug placement mode to Inspect without changing garden state. Automatic browser-local saving and migration remain unchanged; schema 3 preserves old numeric `inventory.leaves` as `inventory.legacyLeaves` of unknown origin. The browser writes to `sandbox:v3`, leaving previous `v2` and `v1` saves untouched as comparison copies. See [the UI control guide](developer-tools.md). GitHub Pages publishes from `main` after the build checks pass.

### Important implemented special cases

- **Flowers:** Daisyspell has a preparation → lighting-change window for the Orchidemon route; Flower-specific Seed identity, sterile outcomes, Leaf curves and compatibility are wired.
- **Aquatic:** Moonlit Lotus → Starlotus uses a prepared Flooded-to-Damp event and preserves valid pre-drain Waterborne contact; Starlotus remembers its Damp/Flooded attunement. Kelpergeist has its Flooded-loss event, and Silvercress has separate innate Growth/Leaf behavior.
- **Woodlands:** Mistlefoe can turn into Mandrabra on *losing* Mistruffle after 12–19 qualifying hours while retaining the other contact, or Mistlewoe at 20 hours with both contacts intact. Mandrakadabra has a four-hour continuous Bright death clock; Bramblewraith tolerates ordinarily lethal extremes with penalties. Bamboozled gives **one Bamboo Steel trophy at or after 100% Growth**, not ordinary Leaves or Seeds; its UI harvest action is gated. Mandrakazam yields Mandrakadabra Seeds.
- **Desert:** All **11 species** and **13 recipes** are active: nine ordinary uninterrupted contact routes and four immediate recovery-event outcomes. Ember Aloe grows **30% faster in Hot** and yields **50% more native Leaves** there; when Hot changes to Warm with real Chillanterelle/Rilllace contact, **fewer than 36 Hot hours → Ashen Rose**, while **36 or more → Aloemancer**. Moonjade is wholly **Dormant under Freezing** while its 48h endurance clock advances; a qualified Freezing → Cold event with Damp, illumination and named contact can create Jadewisp or Pearadox. Pearadox grows in Hot or Freezing with different native output bonuses; Cactaclysm has **48 Hot Leaves and no Seeds**. Mirage Rose's one consuming harvest returns equally many **Ember Aloe and Moonjade Seeds**, never Mirage Rose Seeds. Desert has no Dry-only death timer. This is all source-driven by [§§36 and 74](design/plants/desert.md).
- **Spices:** All **10 species** and **11 recipes** are active, with their separately approved **light-rate/output and moisture-rate/output tables**, compatibility multipliers and hazards. Ten static routes demand uninterrupted resolved conditions and real named-Fungus contact. Sunrice → Pum'king and Dillusion → Pum'king additionally require a reaching **positive environmental Luminous emission from the recipe's named positive group AND a negative environmental Luminous emission from its named negative group**, while the final illumination is **Lit**. A generic Source, adjacency alone or cultivation-only contact cannot replace those fungal emissions. Ghost Rice → Glass Apple instead requires **12h Dark / Damp / Temperate-or-Cold** preparation with Gloamclock/Duskleaf, then a deliberate **exit from Dark**, reaching **Lit within eight elapsed hours** with Prismorel/Lanterncap while the intermediary stays Damp/nonextreme. Ghost Rice, Starberry, Thymekeeper and Glass Apple keep their approved different-species Seed identities; **Pum'king** stays sterile under all Effects. See [§§37–40 and 76](design/plants/spices.md).

These are summaries for locating code, **not compressed substitutes** for the full approved profile details or timing predicates in the linked family documents.

## Differences, deliberate placeholders and unresolved checks

This list distinguishes **provisional engineering values** from approved design. Do not silently promote the following test settings into balance decisions.

| Area | Current implementation / limitation | Where to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fungus lifecycle | All 31 Fungus entries can be placed and use their defined emissions/Effects, but their common **96h Growth + 96h Sporulation**, **8 Spores at 200%**, and **25% germination per Spore** are provisional; final species-by-species timing/economy is not approved. | `src/core/biology.js`, `src/core/economy.js`, `src/core/species.js` |
| Mycelial clock tuning | **12h per colonization step**, **12h unsupported retreat per layer**, **48h Mycelord Flooded death** and **48h dependent-habitat grace** are temporary test values, not signed-off balance. The path/support behavior is implemented. | `src/core/mycelial.js` |
| Rate safety floor | Growth/Ripening uses a positive **0.01× minimum** on the combined fungal rate factor to prevent an unapproved permanent stall; the final floor has not been decided. | `src/core/biology.js` |
| Flowers illumination | Ordinary output varies by approved illumination factors; **natural Flower illumination rate multipliers are neutral** because separate rate values were not approved. This is an explicit non-invention choice, not a general claim that light cannot affect their growth through other mechanics. | `src/core/species.js` |
| Starting inventory / free placement | Six starter Seed stacks of **16** are testing conveniences. Direct Plant/Fungus placement bypasses the sowing economy and, for dependent Fungi, the sowing habitat check; actual sowing still enforces its requirements. | `src/core/grid.js`, `src/App.jsx` |
| Desert Leaf/Seed interpolation | §36 gives exact peaks, seed maxima, signed condition modifiers and some plateaus, but ordinary intermediate curve boundaries are still **prototype interpolations**. Mirage Rose's two Seed halves are rounded down **independently** to preserve an exact 1:1 integer split; a single odd remainder is currently discarded. This rounding convention is an **implementation choice**, not an approved new balance rule. | `src/core/desert-species.js`, `src/core/economy.js` |
| Spices intermediate natural curves | §40 specifies durations, exact peaks, window descriptions and late Seed maxima; several **intermediate Leaf window endpoints, plateau ramp points and linear Seed interpolation** are explicit prototype choices pending visual playtesting, not additional approved balance numbers. They use ordinary production accrual, so light changes affect future increments rather than retroactively re-scaling existing banked output. | `src/core/spices-species.js`, `src/core/economy.js` |
| Legacy pooled Leaf stock | Schema 1–2 saves stored only a **single integer** for Leaves. The original producing species cannot be inferred. Schema 3 preserves that number separately as **unattributed legacy stock** and starts normal per-Plant stacks empty. Its eventual RPG use or exchange treatment is **undecided**; no automatic conversion is permitted. | [Decision](design/decisions/species-specific-leaves.md), `src/core/migration.js`, `src/App.jsx` |
| Player Blockers and garden time | The player-facing UI now offers only Inspect and resource-backed Seed/Spore placement, plus Harvest; **which Blockers players may place is awaiting game design**. Simulation time advances only through developer controls, not an automatic/turn-based game clock. Hiding those controls therefore previews a player UI but does not yet provide an autonomous game loop. | [UI control guide](developer-tools.md), `src/App.jsx`, `src/tool-catalog.js` |
| Spatial inventory and live staging | **Approved, not implemented:** 200 fixed squares in four collapsible pages of 50; one unlimited stack per distinct item; saved manual placement; drag to plant/harvest; quantity popup; reversible inventory reservation before **Apply**; atomic committed planting; delayed concealed germination; no limit on immediately repeated staging phases. **Time keeps advancing while staging** and existing Growth, Transformations, hazards and Mycelial continue visibly updating. Exact hour-per-real-time scale and germination concealment delay remain unresolved. | [Approved specification](design/systems/spatial-inventory-and-staging.md), `src/App.jsx`, `src/core/economy.js` |
| Completion claims | Automated tests exercise registered routes and selected physical layouts, interruptions, persistence, economy and special cases. They do **not** establish exhaustive board-layout feasibility, overall numerical balance, complete art/UI polish, or a production-ready game loop. | `tests/`, [historical design audit](design/audit/README.md) |

## Documentation update policy from this checkpoint

1. **Every gameplay implementation PR** should update this ledger's counts, coverage, implemented exceptions, provisional decisions and verification result; refresh the current-status sentences in the root [README](../README.md) and [design index](design/README.md).
2. The detailed **approved design pages remain the normative requirements except where a later explicit user correction is recorded**, such as the [Plant-specific Leaf item decision](design/decisions/species-specific-leaves.md). Cite exact original sections for unchanged mechanics. Do not rewrite or summarize away older specifications when merely updating implementation progress.
3. Keep the [original full design](herbs-and-fungi-design.md), [environment v1.1](environmental-system-v1.1.md), [environment v1.2](environmental-system-v1.2.md), [handoff](conversation-handoff.md), and [historical/archive audits](design/history/README.md) as preservation evidence. A future *actual design change* needs an explicit decision and separate historical accounting, not a quiet code-driven edit to the approved baseline.
4. Prefer reproducible tests and precise caveats. New functionality is **implemented** only when present in the runtime; mark provisional balance, incomplete geometry checks and manual playtesting separately.
