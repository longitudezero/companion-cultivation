The running game is **Prototype 14 — Save Game / Load Game**. **The full approved first-pass organism and route rosters have been implemented**, and Prototype 11's distinct Leaf inventory, Prototype 12's developer toggle and Prototype 13's live staging are retained. This remains a mechanics-testing garden, **not** a claim of settled balance, exhaustive layout feasibility, art, UI or full release completeness.

**New first-pass playable system:** [200-slot spatial inventory, immediately repeatable staging, Apply, delayed concealed germination and drag-to-harvest](design/systems/spatial-inventory-and-staging.md). Four collapsible pages each contain 50 persistent item positions with an unlimited single stack per item; dragging a Seed/Spore or selecting it and clicking a hex opens a quantity prompt. Uncommitted quantities reserve stock; Apply validates the entire plan at the current environment, spends stock once and privately rolls germination. Identical pending markers reveal living organisms or empty hexes after **six simulated hours (provisional)**. Planning may repeat immediately, while previously committed plots stay locked. A live ticker advances the farm **one simulated hour per five real seconds (provisional)**, independently of staging; existing organisms visibly grow, transform or die. Dragging a living organism to the inventory harvests through the established economy, with the inspector Harvest button retained. Offline progression, balancing and some input/accessibility polish remain open; do not confuse this first pass with finished player progression.

**New player backup functionality:** [Browser autosaving and portable Save Game / Load Game](design/systems/saving-and-backups.md). The header displays the latest browser autosave write status; **Save Game** downloads a versioned `.ccsave` snapshot of all garden data. **Load Game** accepts `.ccsave` and earlier raw `.json`, validates and migrates them **before confirmation**, and replaces the active garden only on consent. Inventory slot positions, current staging reservations, hidden pending outcomes/timers, live organism progress and the simulated clock survive round-trip. A file is local/portable and can be moved between devices; browser autosave remains automatic. The old debug-only raw JSON export/import also remains. No cloud account, automatic offline time catch-up or multi-tab synchronization is implied.

## What is playable now

- **Hex environment:** Moisture, Temperature and Illumination baselines; configurable ordinary/anchored Sources; additive and routed Spread Budget emissions; blocker-aware finite Airborne and connected-Flooded Waterborne reach; environmental adjacency and inspection. These remain the established [environment rules](design/systems/environment.md).
- **Organisms:** Stage owned Seeds or Spores using inventory drag/click controls and commit them with Apply; successful attempts emerge after concealed germination. Turn on **Display developer tools** beside the logo to place any of the 60 registered Plants or 31 registered Fungi for debugging, bypassing sowing. Living Plants advance through Growth (0–100%), Ripening (100–200%) and Mature; Fungi use Growth, Sporulation and Mature. The independent live clock advances deterministic simulation automatically; developer-only `+1 hour` and `+1 day` buttons can skip time. Fungi provide separate environmental emissions and cultivation Effects.
- **Farming economy:** **Each Plant produces a separate named Leaf item** (`inventory.leaves[plantSpecies]`); there is no pooled Leaf currency. Seed/Spore inventories, applied sowing attempts that spend stock, condition- and Effect-sensitive Growth/outputs, time-based output accrual, recipient compatibility, Seed identities, 3:1 net Allocation, one destructive harvest and Spores remain functional. Staging reserves stock without changing inventory until Apply. A Plant's Leaves keep their source identity even if it produces another species' Seeds; Allocation changes quantity, never Leaf type. Fresh gardens start with 16 each of Potato, Daisydaze, Aquatic Base, Woodlands Base, Jadeheart and **Thymely** Seeds; these are **prototype starting quantities**. The interface also offers free placement for test setups without spending stock. Mirage Rose can deliver two Seed species in the same harvest; Spices' different-species Seed identities are also active. A species with no approved plantable Seeds cannot be sown, even if an imported inventory incorrectly contains stock for it.
- **Mycelial ground:** Timed Mycelord expansion; impassable Flooded support connections; selective blockers, overlapping contributors, unsupported retreat; dependent-Fungus sowing requirements and habitat grace; Mycelord Flooded death. This overlay is playable, though its **durations are still provisional**.
- **Transformations:** Real, blocker-aware Fungal contact and resolved Condition checks; per-Plant uninterrupted clocks, fresh destination Plants, transformation/death exact-time tie handling and inspected recipe progress. All six families are registered, including 69 approved routes and event-sensitive transitions. See the tests listed below.
- **Persistence and UI:** Inspect Plant/Fungus stats and recipe eligibility; **see separate Leaf stacks for each producing Plant** in a four-page, 200-slot grid; move/swap saved item positions; stage and move uncommitted Seed/Spore markers; Apply atomically; drag or inspector-harvest. **Save Game**, **Load Game** and an **Autosaved/Autosave unavailable** indicator are now player-facing in the header; debug-only raw JSON Export/Import remains in the inspector. Use a top-bar toggle to reveal/hide developer-only placements and mutations, while keeping Inspect, Sow Seeds, Sow Spores and Harvest available. Inspector baseline editors, Source and Blocker configuration, free deletion, time skipping, demo/blank layout and grid resizing are test-only. Hiding the tools resets an active debug placement mode to Inspect without changing garden state. Schema **4** preserves old numeric `inventory.leaves` as `inventory.legacyLeaves`, adds `inventorySlots`, `staged` and `pending`, and writes browser state to `sandbox:v4`, retaining previous `v3`, `v2` and `v1` saves untouched as comparison copies. A portable file adds a separate **envelope version 1**, not a change to garden schema. See [the UI control guide](developer-tools.md). GitHub Pages publishes from `main` after the build checks pass.

### Important implemented special cases

- **Flowers:** Daisyspell has a preparation → lighting-change window for the Orchidemon route; Flower-specific Seed identity, sterile outcomes, Leaf curves and compatibility are wired.
- **Aquatic:** Moonlit Lotus → Starlotus uses a prepared Flooded-to-Damp event and preserves valid pre-drain Waterborne contact; Starlotus remembers its Damp/Flooded attunement. Kelpergeist has its Flooded-loss event, and Silvercress has separate innate Growth/Leaf behavior.
- **Woodlands:** Mistlefoe can turn into Mandrabra on *losing* Mistruffle after 12–19 qualifying hours while retaining the other contact, or Mistlewoe at 20 hours with both contacts intact. Mandrakadabra has a four-hour continuous Bright death clock; Bramblewraith tolerates ordinarily lethal extremes with penalties. Bamboozled gives **one Bamboo Steel trophy at or after 100% Growth**, not ordinary Leaves or Seeds; its UI harvest action is gated. Mandrakazam yields Mandrakadabra Seeds.
- **Desert:** All **11 species** and **13 recipes** are active: nine ordinary uninterrupted contact routes and four immediate recovery-event outcomes. Ember Aloe grows **30% faster in Hot** and yields **50% more native Leaves** there; when Hot changes to Warm with real Chillanterelle/Rilllace contact, **fewer than 36 Hot hours → Ashen Rose**, while **36 or more → Aloemancer**. Moonjade is wholly **Dormant under Freezing** while its 48h endurance clock advances; a qualified Freezing → Cold event with Damp, illumination and named contact can create Jadewisp or Pearadox. Pearadox grows in Hot or Freezing with different native output bonuses; Cactaclysm has **48 Hot Leaves and no Seeds**. Mirage Rose's one consuming harvest returns equally many **Ember Aloe and Moonjade Seeds**, never Mirage Rose Seeds. Desert has no Dry-only death timer. This is all source-driven by [§§36 and 74](design/plants/desert.md).
- **Spices:** All **10 species** and **11 recipes** are active, with their separately approved **light-rate/output and moisture-rate/output tables**, compatibility multipliers and hazards. Ten static routes demand uninterrupted resolved conditions and real named-Fungus contact. Sunrice → Pum'king and Dillusion → Pum'king additionally require a reaching **positive environmental Luminous emission from the recipe's named positive group AND a negative environmental Luminous emission from its named negative group**, while the final illumination is **Lit**. A generic Source, adjacency alone or cultivation-only contact cannot replace those fungal emissions. Ghost Rice → Glass Apple instead requires **12h Dark / Damp / Temperate-or-Cold** preparation with Gloamclock/Duskleaf, then a deliberate **exit from Dark**, reaching **Lit within eight elapsed hours** with Prismorel/Lanterncap while the intermediary stays Damp/nonextreme. Ghost Rice, Starberry, Thymekeeper and Glass Apple keep their approved different-species Seed identities; **Pum'king** stays sterile under all Effects. See [§§37–40 and 76](design/plants/spices.md).

These are summaries for locating code, **not compressed substitutes** for the full approved profile details or timing predicates in the linked family documents.

## Differences, deliberate placeholders and unresolved checks

This list distinguishes **provisional engineering values** from approved design. Do not silently promote the following test settings into balance decisions.

## Documentation update policy from this checkpoint

1. **Every gameplay implementation PR** should update this ledger's counts, coverage, implemented exceptions, provisional decisions and verification result; refresh the current-status sentences in the root [README](../README.md) and [design index](design/README.md).
2. The detailed **approved design pages remain the normative requirements except where a later explicit user correction is recorded**, such as the [Plant-specific Leaf item decision](design/decisions/species-specific-leaves.md). Cite exact original sections for unchanged mechanics. Do not rewrite or summarize away older specifications when merely updating implementation progress.
3. Keep the [original full design](herbs-and-fungi-design.md), [environment v1.1](environmental-system-v1.1.md), [environment v1.2](environmental-system-v1.2.md), [handoff](conversation-handoff.md), and [historical/archive audits](design/history/README.md) as preservation evidence. A future *actual design change* needs an explicit decision and separate historical accounting, not a quiet code-driven edit to the approved baseline.
4. Prefer reproducible tests and precise caveats. New functionality is **implemented** only when present in the runtime; mark provisional balance, incomplete geometry checks and manual playtesting separately.
