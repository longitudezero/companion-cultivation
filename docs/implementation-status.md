# Companion Cultivation — implementation status

## First tutorial recipe and Carrot Dry grace — IMPLEMENTED (29 October 2026)

To support the planned guided opening on ordinary Dry, Temperate, Dark terrain, **Beanstalk → Carrot** now requires **Dry, Temperate, Dim** with living Prismorel contact for 10 simulated hours; Prismorel's +1 Illumination supplies the final step. Carrot's continuous **Dry** death threshold is now **240 garden hours = 20 real minutes**, giving new players time to learn how to wet the soil after transformation. Other Carrot hazard thresholds remain unchanged. See the [progression design](working/store-and-progression.md) for the agreed **A Thirsty Carrot** follow-up, which will grant Dewmender Spores and teach caring for the transformed Plant.

The changed recipe and hazard are runnable mechanics. **The tutorial tasks, store, automatic Spore rewards, and main progression remain design-only**, not implemented by this balance update.

## Plant naming pass — IMPLEMENTED (29 October 2026)

All 26 approved Plant renames are applied in the runtime registry, 69 Transformation recipes, Seed/Leaf identities, garden inspector, inventory, and Grimoire. The Ingredients family still has **seven** species: former Potato Base is now **Beanstalk** and former terminal Apple is now **Potato**; Cocoa was not added. Profile values, routes, durations and the six-family total of **60** Plants remain unchanged. See the [approved mapping](design/decisions/plant-names.md).

The new garden schema is **V7**. Loading existing **V6** autosaves and portable backups converts old Plant identifiers once, preserving stock, positions, cultivation progress, staged/pending planting, transformation clocks, inventory slots and Grimoire discoveries. Versions V1–V5 remain incompatible with the previous fungal redesign. The browser autosave key is unchanged.

## Temporary Source items — COMPLETE (29 October 2026)

The [working item design](working/temporary-source-items.md) now has a first runtime pass: 26 named catalog entries in seven groups; developer-only item dispenser; individual non-stacking, independently timed Source items in the 200-square inventory; direct item placement, relocation and pickup; active-only countdown and permanent expiry; Range or directional Budget effects through the existing environmental engine. Placing items frees inventory slots. Picking up while the inventory is full is safely refused. Selected Budget items use a floating drag-to-rotate six-way handle. Staged Fungi with environmental or cultivation Budget propagation use the same handle until Apply, after which their heading is fixed in ordinary play.

V6 garden and portable save handling preserves individually identified item instances, placements, headings and remaining time; existing V6 saves without items continue to load. Developer Sources stay configurable and untimed. **Merged to `main` via [PR #26](https://github.com/longitudezero/companion-cultivation/pull/26) and live on GitHub Pages.** The project owner manually verified drag-and-drop to/from inventory and drag-to-rotate in the live game and accepted this phase as complete. Automated validation: **213/213 Node tests**, plus frontend, CommonJS and load smoke builds/tests via `npm run check`. The roster's numerical balance and visual presentation remain first-pass; broader exploratory testing can continue.

**Next phase — store and progression (not yet designed or implemented):** item purchases, currency/economy, progressive access to the seven item groups, and associated player-facing UI. The temporary developer-only dispenser remains until the store is ready.

The old Allocation engine helper is retained but no live Fungus emits Allocation.

**Approved live pace (28 October 2026):** The game now formally uses **one simulated garden hour per five real seconds** while open. This does not rescale or change any stored biological/recipe time values. Player-facing durations, including inspector clocks, timed hazards, germination, event windows, and developer Grimoire recipes, use real minutes/seconds at that pace: 20 game hours displays as **1m 40s**. The developer skip buttons show their equivalent live intervals; individual growth and reveal timers remain playtest-adjustable. Background-tab throttling and closed-page catch-up remain separate questions.

**Verification:** `npm run check` passes **205/205** Node tests plus the CommonJS load smoke test, and builds both Vite and CommonJS outputs. This automated coverage is not a balance signoff or comprehensive manual browser playtest.

The running game is **Prototype 15 — Cultivation Grimoire**. **The full approved first-pass organism and route rosters have been implemented**, and the distinct Leaf inventory, developer toggle, live staging and portable Save Game/Load Game are retained. This remains a mechanics-testing garden, **not** a claim of settled balance, exhaustive layout feasibility, finished illustrations or full release completeness.

**New first playable system:** The [Cultivation Grimoire](design/systems/cultivation-grimoire.md) tracks progressive, **per-garden** discovery of environmental responses, Fungus–Plant effects, hazards and **only actually observed** Transformation witnesses. These facts are shared between Plant and Fungus entries. All six Base Plants and all 31 Fungi begin identified; other Plants stay absent from the book until discovered. The approved V2 bound-book presentation is now integrated: two touching pages, an anchored animated page turn, dark-green cover, lower-corner arrows, top-right close button and eight two-sided colored family/Fungi/Index bookmarks. A jump selector lists only identified entries. Illustration plates and generic descriptions are **placeholders**, not authored species art or final lore. Developer-only inspector recipes remain accessible behind the toggle. **Schema 5** saves the Grimoire with the garden and migrates earlier saves conservatively: identify previously living Plants, never fabricate their old recipe history.

**New first-pass playable system:** [200-slot spatial inventory, immediately repeatable staging, Apply, delayed concealed germination and drag-to-harvest](design/systems/spatial-inventory-and-staging.md). Four collapsible pages each contain 50 persistent item positions with an unlimited single stack per item; dragging a Seed/Spore or selecting it and clicking a hex opens a quantity prompt. Uncommitted quantities reserve stock; Apply validates the entire plan at the current environment, spends stock once and privately rolls germination. Identical pending markers reveal living organisms or empty hexes after **six simulated hours (provisional)**. Planning may repeat immediately, while previously committed plots stay locked. A live ticker advances the farm **one simulated hour per five real seconds (provisional)**, independently of staging; existing organisms visibly grow, transform or die. Dragging a living organism to the inventory harvests through the established economy, with the inspector Harvest button retained. Offline progression, balancing and some input/accessibility polish remain open; do not confuse this first pass with finished player progression.

**New player backup functionality:** [Browser autosaving and portable Save Game / Load Game](design/systems/saving-and-backups.md). The header displays the latest browser autosave write status; **Save Game** downloads a versioned `.ccsave` snapshot of all garden data. **Load Game** accepts `.ccsave` and earlier raw `.json`, validates and migrates them **before confirmation**, and replaces the active garden only on consent. Inventory slot positions, current staging reservations, hidden pending outcomes/timers, live organism progress and the simulated clock survive round-trip. A file is local/portable and can be moved between devices; browser autosave remains automatic. The old debug-only raw JSON export/import also remains. No cloud account, automatic offline time catch-up or multi-tab synchronization is implied.

## What is playable now

- **Hex environment:** Moisture, Temperature and Illumination baselines; configurable ordinary/anchored Sources; additive and routed Spread Budget emissions; blocker-aware finite Airborne and connected-Flooded Waterborne reach; environmental adjacency and inspection. These remain the established [environment rules](design/systems/environment.md).
- **Organisms:** Stage owned Seeds or Spores using inventory drag/click controls and commit them with Apply; successful attempts emerge after concealed germination. Turn on **Display developer tools** beside the logo to place any of the 60 registered Plants or 31 registered Fungi for debugging, bypassing sowing. Living Plants advance through Growth (0–100%), Ripening (100–200%) and Mature; Fungi use Growth, Sporulation and Mature. The independent live clock advances deterministic simulation automatically; developer-only `+1 hour` and `+1 day` buttons can skip time. Fungi provide separate environmental emissions and cultivation Effects.
- **Farming economy:** **Each Plant produces a separate named Leaf item** (`inventory.leaves[plantSpecies]`); there is no pooled Leaf currency. Seed/Spore inventories, applied sowing attempts that spend stock, condition- and Effect-sensitive Growth/outputs, time-based output accrual, recipient compatibility, Seed identities, 3:1 net Allocation, one destructive harvest and Spores remain functional. Staging reserves stock without changing inventory until Apply. A Plant's Leaves keep their source identity even if it produces another species' Seeds; Allocation changes quantity, never Leaf type. Fresh gardens start with 16 each of Beanstalk, Daisydaze, Algea, Moss, Jadeheart and **Thymekeeper** Seeds; these are **prototype starting quantities**. The interface also offers free placement for test setups without spending stock. Everjade can deliver two Seed species in the same harvest; Spices' different-species Seed identities are also active. A species with no approved plantable Seeds cannot be sown, even if an imported inventory incorrectly contains stock for it.
- **Mycelial ground:** Timed Mycelord expansion; impassable Flooded support connections; selective blockers, overlapping contributors, unsupported retreat; dependent-Fungus sowing requirements and habitat grace; Mycelord Flooded death. This overlay is playable, though its **durations are still provisional**.
- **Transformations:** Real, blocker-aware Fungal contact and resolved Condition checks; per-Plant uninterrupted clocks, fresh destination Plants, transformation/death exact-time tie handling and **developer-only** recipe-progress inspection. All six families are registered, including 69 approved routes and event-sensitive transitions. Successful events update the Grimoire with the actually witnessed pathway, not alternative possibilities.
- **Persistence and UI:** Inspect growing Plant/Fungus stats; **see separate Leaf stacks for each Plant** in a four-page, 200-slot grid; move/swap saved items; stage Seed/Spore markers; Apply; drag or inspector-harvest. The player header includes **❦ Cultivation Grimoire**, **Save Game**, **Load Game** and autosave status. Species-specific undiscovered recipe descriptions, Fungal effect magnitude and transformation clocks are now **developer-only**; the ordinary How it works guide explains experimentation without giving away unseen pathways. Developer baseline editors, unrestricted placement, configuration, removal, clock skips and garden resizing stay behind the existing toggle. Schema **5** adds `grimoire` to the established Plant-keyed Leaf inventory, `inventorySlots`, `staged`, `pending` and `mycelium` state; browser writes move to `sandbox:v5` and retain previous `v1`–`v4` keys untouched. The portable backup envelope remains **version 1**, distinct from the garden schema. See [the UI control guide](developer-tools.md). GitHub Pages publishes from `main` after the build checks pass.

### Important implemented special cases

- **Flowers:** Orchiwitch has a preparation → lighting-change window for the Orchidemon route; Flower-specific Seed identity, sterile outcomes, Leaf curves and compatibility are wired.
- **Aquatic:** Moonlit Lotus → Starlotus uses a prepared Flooded-to-Damp event and preserves valid pre-drain Waterborne contact; Starlotus remembers its Damp/Flooded attunement. Kelpergeist has its Flooded-loss event, and Silvercress has separate innate Growth/Leaf behavior.
- **Woodlands:** Mistlefoe can turn into Mandrabra on *losing* Mistruffle after 12–19 qualifying hours while retaining the other contact, or Mistlewoe at 20 hours with both contacts intact. Mandrakadabra has a four-hour continuous Bright death clock; Bramblewraith tolerates ordinarily lethal extremes with penalties. Bamboozled gives **one Bamboo Steel trophy at or after 100% Growth**, not ordinary Leaves or Seeds; its UI harvest action is gated. Mandrakazam yields Mandrakadabra Seeds.
- **Desert:** All **11 species** and **13 recipes** are active: nine ordinary uninterrupted contact routes and four immediate recovery-event outcomes. Aloechemist grows **30% faster in Hot** and yields **50% more native Leaves** there; when Hot changes to Warm with real Chillanterelle/Rilllace contact, **fewer than 36 Hot hours → Cactastrophe**, while **36 or more → Aloemancer**. Moonjade is wholly **Dormant under Freezing** while its 48h endurance clock advances; a qualified Freezing → Cold event with Damp, illumination and named contact can create Jadewisp or Pearadox. Pearadox grows in Hot or Freezing with different native output bonuses; Cactacomb has **48 Hot Leaves and no Seeds**. Everjade's one consuming harvest returns equally many **Aloechemist and Moonjade Seeds**, never Everjade Seeds. Desert has no Dry-only death timer. This is all source-driven by [§§36 and 74](design/plants/desert.md).
- **Spices:** All **10 species** and **11 recipes** are active, with their separately approved **light-rate/output and moisture-rate/output tables**, compatibility multipliers and hazards. Ten static routes demand uninterrupted resolved conditions and real named-Fungus contact. Oreganoir → Papriking and Wyrmsalt → Papriking additionally require a reaching **positive environmental Luminous emission from the recipe's named positive group AND a negative environmental Luminous emission from its named negative group**, while the final illumination is **Lit**. A generic Source, adjacency alone or cultivation-only contact cannot replace those fungal emissions. Saffrune → Phantarragon instead requires **12h Dark / Damp / Temperate-or-Cold** preparation with Gloamclock/Duskleaf, then a deliberate **exit from Dark**, reaching **Lit within eight elapsed hours** with Prismorel/Lanterncap while the intermediary stays Damp/nonextreme. Saffrune, Cinnamancer, Mighty Mint and Phantarragon keep their approved different-species Seed identities; **Papriking** stays sterile under all Effects. See [§§37–40 and 76](design/plants/spices.md).

These are summaries for locating code, **not compressed substitutes** for the full approved profile details or timing predicates in the linked family documents.
