## Guild Shop — IMPLEMENTED (31 October 2026)

The third header icon opens a forest/parchment Buy/Sell overlay, with the same manual speed controls as the Taskboard. Beanstalk Seeds are always free, starter/task-unlocked Spores cost 2 Gold each, and species-specific Leaves sell for 1 Gold each. Stock is unlimited. Product selection shows owned quantities, quantity controls, total cost/proceeds, resulting balance and a receipt. Only Leaves can be sold; no Seed/Spore/trophy/Source-item or unattributed legacy Leaf sales are added.

Trades and task rewards share `shop.gold`. Old task balances migrate once without duplicate credit; old sandboxes can trade without activating the tutorial. Spore access follows guild rewards, persists after depletion, and does not follow the complete Grimoire reference. Legacy sandboxes retain acquired Fungi access. Autosaves, portable saves and plot resizing preserve wallet and unlocks. Selling immediately updates current-stock delivery readiness. Insufficient Gold, invalid quantities, overflow and full-inventory failures leave all state intact; existing stacks can be topped up in a full inventory.

**Validation:** all 297 Node tests, production frontend/CommonJS builds and the CommonJS smoke test pass. Coverage includes shared reward/trade balances, unlock/depletion recovery, Leaf-only sales, delivery-readiness reversal, atomic failure, safe quantities, exact legacy Gold migration and persistence. Desktop playthrough accompanies deployment.

Expansion/Blocker/Source-item products and differentiated pricing remain deferred. A concrete balance gap remains: free Seeds alone cannot guarantee zero-Gold/no-Fungi recovery because Beanstalks in untouched Dry/Dark soil die before Leaf production. This pass preserves agreed biology and prices; see [shop rules](working/shop-first-pass.md).

## What is playable now

- **Hex environment:** Moisture, Temperature and Illumination baselines; configurable ordinary/anchored Sources; additive and routed Spread Budget emissions; blocker-aware finite Airborne and connected-Flooded Waterborne reach; environmental adjacency and inspection. These remain the established [environment rules](design/systems/environment.md).
- **Organisms:** Stage owned Seeds or Spores using inventory drag/click controls and commit them with Apply; successful attempts emerge after concealed germination. Turn on **Display developer tools** beside the logo to place any of the 60 registered Plants or 31 registered Fungi for debugging, bypassing sowing. Living Plants advance through Growth (0–100%), Ripening (100–200%) and Mature; Fungi use Growth, Sporulation and Mature. The independent live clock advances deterministic simulation automatically; developer-only `+1 hour` and `+1 day` buttons can skip time. Fungi provide separate environmental emissions and cultivation Effects.
- **Farming economy:** **Each Plant produces a separate named Leaf item** (`inventory.leaves[plantSpecies]`); there is no pooled Leaf currency. Seed/Spore inventories, applied sowing attempts that spend stock, condition- and Effect-sensitive Growth/outputs, time-based output accrual, recipient compatibility, Seed identities, 3:1 net Allocation, one destructive harvest and Spores remain functional. Staging reserves stock without changing inventory until Apply. A Plant's Leaves keep their source identity even if it produces another species' Seeds; Allocation changes quantity, never Leaf type. Fresh gardens start with 16 each of Beanstalk, Daisydaze, Algea, Moss, Jadeheart and **Thymekeeper** Seeds; these are **prototype starting quantities**. The interface also offers free placement for test setups without spending stock. Everjade can deliver two Seed species in the same harvest; Spices' different-species Seed identities are also active. A species with no approved plantable Seeds cannot be sown, even if an imported inventory incorrectly contains stock for it.
- **Mycelial ground:** Timed Mycelord expansion; impassable Flooded support connections; selective blockers, overlapping contributors, unsupported retreat; dependent-Fungus sowing requirements and habitat grace; Mycelord Flooded death. This overlay is playable, though its **durations are still provisional**.
- **Transformations:** Real, blocker-aware Fungal contact and resolved Condition checks; per-Plant uninterrupted clocks, fresh destination Plants, transformation/death exact-time tie handling and **developer-only** recipe-progress inspection. All six families are registered, including 69 approved routes and event-sensitive transitions. Successful events update the Grimoire with the actually witnessed pathway, not alternative possibilities.
- **Persistence and UI:** Inspect growing Plant/Fungus stats; **see separate Leaf stacks for each Plant** in a four-page, 200-slot grid; move/swap saved items; stage Seed/Spore markers; Apply; drag or inspector-harvest. The player header includes **❦ Cultivation Grimoire**, **Save Game**, **Load Game** and autosave status. Species-specific undiscovered recipe descriptions, Fungal effect magnitude and transformation clocks are now **developer-only**; the ordinary How it works guide explains experimentation without giving away unseen pathways. Developer baseline editors, unrestricted placement, configuration, removal, clock skips and garden resizing stay behind the existing toggle. Schema **5** adds `grimoire` to the established Plant-keyed Leaf inventory, `inventorySlots`, `staged`, `pending` and `mycelium` state; browser writes move to `sandbox:v5` and retain previous `v1`–`v4` keys untouched. The portable backup envelope remains **version 1**, distinct from the garden schema. See [the UI control guide](developer-tools.md). GitHub Pages publishes from `main` after the build checks pass.

### Important implemented special cases

- **Flowers:** Orchiwitch has a preparation → lighting-change window for the Orchidemon route; Flower-specific Seed identity, sterile outcomes, Leaf curves and compatibility are wired.
- **Aquatic:** Moonlit Lotus → Starlotus uses a prepared Flooded-to-Damp event and preserves valid pre-drain Waterborne contact; Starlotus remembers its Damp/Flooded attunement. Kelpergeist has its Flooded-loss event, and Silvercress has separate innate Growth/Leaf behavior.
- **Woodlands:** Mistlefoe can turn into Mandrabra on *losing* Mistruffle after 12–19 qualifying hours while retaining the other contact, or Mistlewoe at 20 hours with both contacts intact. Mandrakadabra has a four-hour continuous Bright death clock; Bramblewraith tolerates ordinarily lethal extremes with penalties. Bamboozled gives **one Bamboo Steel trophy at or after 100% Growth**, not ordinary Leaves or Seeds; its UI harvest action is gated. Mandrakazam yields Mandrakadabra Seeds.
- **Desert:** All **11 species** and **13 recipes** are active: nine ordinary uninterrupted contact routes and four immediate recovery-event outcomes. Aloechemist grows **30% faster in Hot** and yields **50% more native Leaves** there; when Hot changes to Warm with real Chillanterelle/Rilllace contact, **fewer than 36 Hot hours → Cactastrophe**, while **36 or more → Aloemancer**. Moonjade is wholly **Dormant under Freezing** while its 48h endurance clock advances; a qualified Freezing → Cold event with Damp, illumination and named contact can create Jadewisp or Pearadox. Pearadox grows in Hot or Freezing with different native output bonuses; Cactacomb has **48 Hot Leaves and no Seeds**. Everjade's one consuming harvest returns equally many **Aloechemist and Moonjade Seeds**, never Everjade Seeds. Desert has no Dry-only death timer. This is all source-driven by [§§36 and 74](design/plants/desert.md).
- **Spices:** All **10 species** and **11 recipes** are active, with their separately approved **light-rate/output and moisture-rate/output tables**, compatibility multipliers and hazards. Ten static routes demand uninterrupted resolved conditions and real named-Fungus contact. Oreganoir → Papriking and Wyrmsalt → Papriking additionally require a reaching **positive environmental Luminous emission from the recipe's named positive group AND a negative environmental Luminous emission from its named negative group**, while the final illumination is **Lit**. A generic Source, adjacency alone or cultivation-only contact cannot replace those fungal emissions. Saffrune → Phantarragon instead requires **12h Dark / Damp / Temperate-or-Cold** preparation with Gloamclock/Duskleaf, then a deliberate **exit from Dark**, reaching **Lit within eight elapsed hours** with Prismorel/Lanterncap while the intermediary stays Damp/nonextreme. Saffrune, Cinnamancer, Mighty Mint and Phantarragon keep their approved different-species Seed identities; **Papriking** stays sterile under all Effects. See [§§37–40 and 76](design/plants/spices.md).

These are summaries for locating code, **not compressed substitutes** for the full approved profile details or timing predicates in the linked family documents.
