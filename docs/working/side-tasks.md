# Side tasks

> **PLAYABLE TEST · 31 October 2026.** One basic Leaf delivery is implemented to test the Side section, multiple pins, and explicit resource handoff. Its 10 Gold reward is provisional.

Use the same fields as [main tasks](main-tasks.md), with an **S-** ID and **no Order field**. Keep **Speaker**, **Description**, and **Completion dialogue** within each task record. Field definitions and the reusable template live in [Task organization and progression](task-organization.md).

The [guild setting and task voice](../design/setting-and-task-voice.md) contains example request ideas for future side tasks. Those examples are not yet task records with approved objectives or rewards.

## A Few Carrot Leaves — S-5CRTLF

- **Speaker:** Guild Cook.
- **Prerequisite:** Claim My First Transformation. Available alongside A Thirsty Carrot; completing the care task is not required.
- **Description:** “I need five Carrot Leaves for tonight’s soup. The Carrots can keep their roots. I’m only asking for the leafy bits.”
- **Objective:** Give 5 Carrot Leaves. Any currently held Carrot Leaves count, including earlier harvests. Other species and unattributed legacy Leaves do not count.
- **Submission:** Complete task consumes exactly five Leaves and grants the reward together. Readiness follows current stock and disappears if the player spends below five. No automatic delivery or partial handoff.
- **Reward:** 10 Gold, once; a playtest balance value.
- **Completion:** “Five Carrot Leaves. Just what the soup needed. Here’s your Gold. Try not to spend it all on more soup.”
- **Guidance:** Check the Inspector’s Leaf yield before destructive harvesting. The full board shows held progress and explains that completion consumes the Leaves.
- **Pinning:** Optional, independent of progression. It can appear alongside the main care task in the tracker.
