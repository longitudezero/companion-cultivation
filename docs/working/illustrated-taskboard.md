# Illustrated Taskboard and interface

**Implemented · 1 November 2026.** The supplied mockup is a visual guideline; only the existing two main tasks and one side delivery remain playable.

- Main Tasks, Side Tasks and Completed Tasks appear in that order in a single parchment list. All three groups collapse independently and retain their headings/counts. Completed begins collapsed unless history is the initial selection. Empty groups explain their availability. Selecting a pinned request opens its entry and expands its section.
- A fulfilled objective has a green checkmark; unfinished objectives have an empty box. These are status indicators, not manually selectable inputs. Ready to complete remains distinct from Completed, which requires claiming on the board and showing the reward receipt.
- List rows have a species thumbnail, title, current status, pin state and compact progress. Delivery requests use current held quantities; completed deliveries retain their completed history after consuming Leaves.
- The explanation is wider than the list. Its title, task-type badge, rewards/progress/actions and four corner ornaments stay fixed while the descriptive middle scrolls. The list scrolls inside fixed left-side ornaments, with no right-side corner art. Narrow layouts stack the panels with bounded scrolling areas.
- Gold rewards use the supplied coins; Spore rewards keep each Fungus's existing illustration, name and quantity. Expansion artwork is available for later actual rewards; this presentation pass adds no example rewards or gameplay tasks from the mockup.
- The supplied Shop and Taskboard illustrations replace the code-drawn header icons. Current Moisture, Temperature and Illumination icons appear beside Inspector labels/values, preserving fixed widths. Every Environment segment uses the corresponding garden palette color, with an outline/marker on the current resolved condition. Leaf/Seed/Spore yields use the supplied generic resource artwork.
- The orange thermometer maps to Warm and the red/fire thermometer to Hot. Freezing, Cold and Temperate keep their names. There is no added Scorching condition.
- The supplied Back button CSS is adapted to a green/gold badge: a small hover lift, warm rim light, arrow response and slight press scale. Generic page resets, glassy white highlights, global contrast and large z-index values are omitted. Keyboard focus gets the same feedback, reduced motion removes movement, Escape closes, and focus returns to the launcher.
- Original PNG/WebP assets are copied without altering pixels. SVG view boxes normalize visible drawing size and select badge/corner regions from the shared sheets, keeping source art reproducible. See `public/ui/README.md` and `src/ui/GameArt.jsx`.
- The Back arrow has no circular backing. Complete task uses the lower-right supplied badge, centered below the thicker gold-rimmed progress bar. Its runes are decorative, translucent and softly glowing; hover adds a glass reflection, with no motion under reduced-motion preferences.
- A rotating pin icon sits left of the category badge and retains its accessible label and pressed state. Six distinct fixed corner designs sit outward over the heavier panel borders; the list still has only left corners.
- New tutorial garden is beside Save/Load in the header. A separate confirmation dialog offers Cancel before replacing the garden, traps keyboard focus and supports Escape. The Taskboard no longer contains the reset action.
- The existing Carrot Dry exposure timer keeps its actual simulation values and now uses the warning illustration plus a bright red, gold-rimmed rune bar. No exposure, death or reward rules change.
- The next polish pass moves corners another 3px outward and deepens panel borders to antique gold. Complete task gains the Back button's hover/focus lift, warm halo, label response and press scale; disabled buttons stay still.
- Section headings use decorative emerald ribbons with two identical glow fields scrolling sideways over 34 seconds, adapted from the supplied `fantasy_bars_animated.html`. They have no progress semantics or values; real counts and collapse controls remain readable. Reduced motion stops the drift.
- Task progress uses a darker emerald fill, a 3px gold rim and capsule ends. Germination now shares the rune-bar component and renders Germinating… above the runes with a dark text shadow. Death exposure uses blood-red shading without orange highlights. Progress values, reveal timing and hidden-outcome rules remain unchanged.
- Inventory and Shop stock areas have fixed, faint pale-gold ivy behind the slots and unused space. A render-time tonal filter preserves the supplied transparent artwork's shading; it does not alter the originals. Empty inventory squares have a translucent backing so the watermark remains visible, while occupied cards retain their distinct type colors and readable labels. Decorative layers ignore pointer input and are hidden from accessibility.
- Taskboard and Shop share a centered header with pale-gold ivy at the upper left. Equal side columns center the title across the modal, with Gold and the Back button together at the right. Smaller layouts put the controls on a separate row to keep the title centered and prevent overlap.
- The next control pass removes speed controls from both overlays; the garden owns the single speed control group. Both overlays now use the same animated Back badge, and their headers include pale-gold ivy at both sides. Opening or closing an overlay leaves speed unchanged.
- Garden speed buttons use the supplied Pause/0.5×/1×/2× artwork with pressed-state emphasis, hover/focus lift and warm glow, press feedback and reduced-motion support. Accessible names and clock semantics are unchanged.

- Growth & Harvest places each resource amount directly after its icon and before the label. Leaf, Seed and Spore peak markers use the corresponding artwork; separate lanes retain readable coincident peaks and all existing landmark tooltips/timing.

## Main menu and optional staging

- Remove the top bar. Main menu, Grimoire, Taskboard and Shop live in a compact vertical rail on the Inspector’s right. A code-native green/gold botanical menu icon joins the supplied launchers; hover, focus and reduced-motion behavior remain consistent. The mobile Inventory button stays available beside garden controls.
- The logo heads a native modal menu with New garden, Load Game, Save Game, Display/Hide developer tools in that order. Staging, elapsed time and autosave status follow; How it works sits at the foot. The native dialog keeps background playfield input inert and traps focus; Back/Escape returns to the launcher. New garden retains its existing confirmation.
- Staging defaults on and is remembered separately as a browser preference. Turn it off to consume and plant a single placement on quantity confirmation, without a separate Apply. Existing germination time, concealed roll, inventory, habitat and discovery rules remain canonical. Existing proposals must be applied or cancelled before disabling staging; loading a saved plan enables staging to keep it editable.
- The staging action bar is present only while proposals exist, including after Undo or Cancel. Pending germinations alone do not keep it open.
- Quantity selection produces an ephemeral preview. Budget Fungi show the same six-direction drag handle while the selector remains open, with a direction dropdown and keyboard arrows as alternatives. Preview rotation never reserves or spends stock; Cancel/Escape/navigation discards it. Confirm commits its selected heading in either planting mode.
