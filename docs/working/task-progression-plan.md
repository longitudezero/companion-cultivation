**Working plan · 11 November 2026.** Records the agreed progression changes for the current task drafts. Task names, permanent IDs, quantities and rewards are finalized in the [main](main-tasks.md) and [side](side-tasks.md) task records as each task is reviewed.

## Mycelial ground

Teach the rule directly in the task and Inspector. Mycelial ground slows plant growth and ripening, and prolonged exposure can kill most Plants. Some Fungi gain a stronger cultivation effect when they grow on it. A Plant on uncolonized ground can receive that effect if the Fungus reaches it.

| Placement | Objective and feedback | Reward or availability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **14.1 · Something’s Growing Under There**, after Task 14 grants Mycelord Spores | Establish a living Mycelord supporting three connected Mycelial hexes, including its own. Show the colony’s footprint and warn about plant exposure as it spreads. | Keep the draft Spore and Gold rewards. Open the boost practice. |
| **14.2 · Use a boosted fungus**, after 14.1 | Establish Emberbolete on supported Mycelial ground and apply its cultivation effect to one living Plant on uncolonized ground. Show Emberbolete’s Seed Yield effect increasing from +10% to +30% in the Inspector. | Award a small bundle of [Root Barriers](player-blockers.md). Open Tasks 15–18 and **Contain the spread**. |
| **Contain the spread**, optional side task after the boost practice | Use the rewarded Blockers to stop a supported colony’s advance toward a living crop. Keep a living Mycelord supporting part of the colony and the crop on uncolonized ground. Highlight the protected boundary and any route the colony can still use. | Available alongside the four projects. Task reward TBD. |

Flooded ground can also stop spread. If the player cuts every support route to an already colonized area, show that area losing support and retreating gradually. Teach containment with a living crop and an active colony.

The Woodlands project applies the boost lesson with Mistruffle reaching Charmomile. Its Siltgill reward introduces a Fungus that depends on Mycelial ground.

The [player Blocker catalogue](player-blockers.md) defines the approved items and their filters. **Contain the spread** depends on the player being able to claim and place the Task 14.2 Root Barriers. That claim unlocks the Edge and Hex Root Barriers in the Shop. Final bundle size and the first reward tasks for other designs are part of the main-task reward review.

## Capacity and rewards

Keep the first garden row at **Task 5** and the first ten Inventory slots at **Task 7**. Their claims unlock the corresponding [Shop upgrades](task-organization.md#garden-and-inventory-capacity--agreed). Move the later draft capacity rewards to Shop purchases. Use main-task reward slots for useful Blockers and temporary Source items alongside the Seeds and Spores that support the next assignment.

Use surplus Leaf sales as the main source of Gold for expansion. Playtest the first purchase after the free row and Inventory slots. Deliver Task 8’s 24 Leaves across Rice, Strawberry, and Onion, sell surplus from the same growing setups, and account for routine Seed and Spore restocking. Check whether the player can afford enough space for late Flowers and the four family projects without extra harvest cycles devoted solely to the purchase. If the player is close, tune the first upgrade price before adding a task Gold payout.

## Family specializations

- **Aquatic, Tasks 20–20.2.** Task 20 introduces Moonlit Lotus through Sunken Lotus. The path continues through preparing Moonlit Lotus in Flooded, Cold, Dim conditions with Rainmorel, draining its hex to Damp once preparation is complete, and harvesting the resulting Starlotus. Show a clear ready-to-drain signal when preparation completes. Use the Fungi available on the Aquatic route so players can finish this specialization independently. Reserve the Kelpergeist route to Starlotus for *Two Ways to Grow a Miracle* (Task 29).
- **Woodlands, Task 21.2.** Keep the rescue of the same Mandrakadabra the player just transformed. Prompt the player to prepare a way to lower illumination before triggering the transformation. Surface the remaining Bright exposure time as soon as Mandrakadabra appears. Playtest the current four-garden-hour window at 1× and 2× (20 and 10 real seconds respectively). Lengthen it if a prepared player still needs to watch the hex continuously to respond in time.

## Leaf production after the family unlocks

Make **Task 19** the first bulk Leaf order after the four family projects. Put the higher counts on renewable crops and rebalance the draft counts against actual per-species harvests and garden layouts.

Set quantities high enough that establishing and containing a Mycelial colony for Leaf Yield boosts becomes a serious production choice. A good boosted layout should save noticeable growing time or garden space. Playtest representative ordinary and boosted layouts at 1×, including harvest windows, and tune the orders so a player can meet them in a reasonable number of harvests without hours of repetition. Keep total quantity and species variety as separate ways to raise difficulty.

For **Task 24, the Grand Exhibition**, keep each seedless Plant’s quota within one ordinary successful harvest from its specialization, including Starlotus and Mandrakadabra. Use renewable crops for larger quantities. Continue accepting earlier harvests for deliveries. Preview the Exhibition’s Leaf requests as the relevant paths open so the player can plan production and save Leaves along the way.
