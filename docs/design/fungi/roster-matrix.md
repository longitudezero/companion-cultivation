# Approved first-pass Fungi — 31-species parameter matrix

> **Editorial audit reference, not a new balance decision.** This matrix cross-checks each approved fungus's two separate outputs against detailed source §§47–50,53–55,57 and later approved global rules (§§56,60–65). The unabridged [foundational](profiles-foundation.md), [alternative](profiles-alternatives.md), and [final](profiles-final.md) profiles remain the detailed reference. Original repository documentation has not been changed.

**Notation:** **R1/R2** means Airborne Range 1/2; **pp** means lifecycle percentage points, not percent increase. **Directed Spread Budget** is the implemented routed mode, not an ordinary Range. The environmental emission and Plant cultivation emission of the same fungus have **independent propagation and Blocker reach**. An emitted cultivation Effect applies only to a receiving Plant eligible for that Effect, but a named-contact Transformation may inspect its actual geometric footprint regardless of lifecycle-stage applicability (§68).

## Cross-cutting qualifiers — do not lose these when coding

1. **Waterborne (§56):** Tidegill, Sunspore, Rilllace, Brookrill and Siltgill deliver their listed *cultivation* Influences through the entire reachable connected Flooded component, not a three-hex radius. A non-Flooded fungus source may inject into an adjacent Flooded hex; subsequent traversal must follow connected Flooded terrain and respect blockers. Their separate Airborne *environmental* emissions retain the ordinary Ranges above.
2. **Allocation (§56):** Frostfolio and Bristlecap exchange eligible Seeds into Leaves; Sunspore and Goldgill exchange eligible Leaves into valid Plant Seeds. Their nominal donor fractions are **80%, 60%, 80%, 60%**, respectively. The fixed first-pass unit conversion is **3 Seeds ↔ 1 Leaf** at the **single destructive Plant harvest**, subject to both-stream eligibility, signed net stacking, compatibility, clamping and integer rounding. Frostfolio's source-side Mycelial multiplier is specifically **1.25×**, not a general bonus.
3. **Mycelial dependence (§60C):** Rushroom and Siltgill need Mycelial on *their fungus source hex* to establish. If source support is lost later, they stay alive **and emit both normal Influences** through the continuous species-specific X-hour grace timer; restoration clears that timer, and expiry removes the Source. The X-hour values are still unassigned. Plants reached outside the Mycelial overlay can benefit from the emission and can meet a named Transformation contact.
4. **Mycelord (§§61–63):** Only this initial-roster species creates the timed Mycelial overlay. It has no ordinary cultivation/environmental Influence and cannot satisfy a named Fungus contact by adjacency or overlay alone. Flooded cuts colony support immediately and starts Mycelord's own X-hour death exposure, while previously established colony contributions retreat with elapsed simulation time.
5. **Time-based production (§§60,65):** Echomorel, Sim'mushro'dim, Duskleaf and Brookrill accrue output only while the named Source reaches a Plant **and progress advances** inside the source's exact numeric window. Source-compatible ordinary Yield and environmental output are prospective; harvest-time Allocation is a separate step.
6. **Germination:** Hushroom (+8 pp/Seed) and Dewmender (+4 pp/Seed) act when a Plant Seed stack is **planted**, not after the Plant starts growing. They modify the target Seed species' per-Seed chance under its compatibility and the existing additive cap; they do not automatically modify Fungus Spore planting.
7. **No added Seed identities or blooms:** Seed-shifting and Seed-producing fungi do not alter a Plant's approved Seed species or turn intrinsically sterile Plants fertile. Timing/Window modifiers reshape existing natural curves rather than creating a fungus-added second bloom.

## Parameters genuinely not settled in the source

- Exact fungus Growth/Sporulation days, own-Spore quantities, Spore germination chances, detailed vitality and most species-specific survival/habitat lethal-hour clocks.
- Source-side Mycelial bonus magnitudes for Echomorel and Sim'mushro'dim; optional Mycelial-preferring status for Emberbolete/Chillanterelle was only a **test candidate**, not approved as an extra current bonus.
- Some exact continuous curve interpolation and environmental/biological numerical tuning after actual garden playtests. **Do not fill those gaps by extrapolating from profile examples.**
