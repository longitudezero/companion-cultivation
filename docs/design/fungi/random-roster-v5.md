> **Status: WORKING EXPERIMENT — NOT APPROVED OR PLAYABLE.** This pass retains the [V4 cultivation and environmental assignments](random-roster-v4.md) exactly and explores *source-side Mycelial-dependence*. Previous V0–V4 draft versions are preserved. **Mycelord remains unchanged** as the only initial creator of Mycelial ground.

## User's new direction

- Make **one** of the two + Leaf Yield Fungi Mycelial-dependent.
- Make **one** + Hourly Leaves Fungus Mycelial-dependent (only F10 currently offers that effect).
- Recommend any additional dependent Fungi based on the *complete roster*, retaining non-Mycelial strategic alternatives.
- No cultivation or environmental effects, strengths, names, modes, ranges or Plant recipes were edited in this pass.

## Recommendation: start with three dependents out of twelve

| Fungus | Dependency decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **F09** | **Dependent — proposed Leaf Yield choice** | F02 stays available as the other + Leaf Yield route without requiring Mycelial. F09 has + Leaf Yield alongside a shorter Harvest Window and +2 Warming; its eventual Yield strength can justify the infrastructure investment. |
| **F10** | **Dependent — the one Hourly Leaves source** | Required by the user's chosen category. It offers a unique hourly-Leaf route and also + Growth Rate with a shorter Harvest Window, making dependence meaningful. |
| **F11** | **Recommended additional dependent — not yet confirmed** | F11 combines + Seed Yield and + Germination with earlier Leaf peak timing. F03 remains an independent candidate for + Seed Yield; this differentiates a more demanding F11 from an accessible F03. |

**Why stop at three for now:** The prior 31-Fungus roster had only **two** strictly source-dependent species (Rushroom, Siltgill), plus preferring species. Three of twelve is already a substantial increase in the proportion locked behind Mycelial infrastructure. Do not add dependence just because a Fungus seems powerful in isolation.

**In particular, keep F12's unique + Hourly Seeds independent for now:** If both F10 and F12 were dependent, *every hourly production method in this candidate roster* would require a Mycelial colony. A Seed Yield route remains outside Mycelial through F03, and a Leaf Yield route remains through F02.

**F07 as a separate future possibility:** It bundles + Germination, longer Harvest Window and slower Ripening, potentially supporting hourly production. Consider *Mycelial-preferring* rather than dependent as a softer synergy, but **do not assign any preference strength or status yet**.

## What “dependent” means here

Under the current approved Mycelial system:

1. **Mycelial must be on the Fungus's own occupied source hex to establish.** The target Plant does *not* have to be planted on Mycelial ground.
2. Losing Mycelial support later does not instantly turn off the dependent Fungus. It remains alive and emits normally during its species-specific continuous X-hour habitat-loss grace period. The timer is reset if support returns; it dies when grace expires.
3. **Only Mycelord creates initial Mycelial ground**, through time-based spread and retreat. Flooded ground interrupts new spread and support paths.
4. Actual Mycelial grace durations, source reach/modes, final dependency membership, and any source-side preference boost are not assigned by V5.

The current playable 31-Fungus roster, Mycelord, 69 transformations, code, saves and Grimoire have not changed.
